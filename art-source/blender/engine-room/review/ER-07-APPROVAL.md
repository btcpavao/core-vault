## Review summary

The current Blender scene is unmistakably the same designed environment as the approved Hero Reference and is sufficiently complete to stop source-art production and begin measured optimization.

- Composition: PASS. The central Reactor authority, floor/ceiling balance, secondary chamber relationship, console hierarchy, and architectural framing are stable and intentional.
- Architecture: PASS. Columns, arches, ceiling members, platform, and room shell read as coherent three-dimensional construction from the intended alternate views rather than as a hero-camera facade.
- Reactor: PASS. The main asset has a strong silhouette, organized mechanical density, readable internal layers, physically plausible ports, installed conduits, and a related secondary chamber. No placeholder or obvious shading defect remains at intended inspection distance.
- Materials: PASS. Limestone, dark engineering metal, bronze, glass, floor, and console form a coherent physical palette. Remaining offline-render nuance is non-blocking.
- Lighting: PASS. Warm left daylight, cooler fill, dimensional shadows, bronze readability, and interior depth preserve the approved calm and serious hierarchy.
- Energy: PASS. Controlled blue energy is layered through the internal machinery and subordinate in the secondary chamber. It remains restrained rather than over-bloomed.
- Exterior: PASS. Sea, distant land, atmosphere, and horizon support Mediterranean depth without taking focus from the Reactor. The efficient single-plane matte is acceptable for the intended cameras.
- Console: PASS. The enclosure reads as a finished physical secondary asset. The standby screen correctly avoids claiming runtime truth.
- Diminishing returns: PASS. Another Blender production pass would primarily chase microtexture, photographic optics, and volumetric nuance that must be reconsidered during real-time translation.

The `er-06b-vs-er-07.png` comparison contains no visual change. Decoded hero pixels are identical with MD5 `fe637178cb8606a5d533ef07b8ea2359`. ER-07 changed no production art.

## Accepted non-blocking differences

- The reference has denser stone microtexture, richer roughness variation, subtler bronze wear, and more photographic glass response.
- The Blender energy is layered geometry with localized light interaction rather than full participating-media volume.
- The distant exterior is a packed single-plane matte and therefore has no physical parallax outside intended views.
- Some generated reference micro-machinery and cable density are intentionally simplified; the authored Reactor still holds up at the required inspection distance.
- The canonical reference contains presentation UI and a bottom state strip. The Blender Final Hero intentionally contains no UI overlay, and the console remains in a static standby state because runtime owns application truth.
- The reference has stronger photographic post-processing than the restrained Blender EEVEE result.

These differences do not damage visual identity, architecture, material language, focal hierarchy, engineering seriousness, Mediterranean atmosphere, or perceived source-art completeness.
